About TheSmartGreen

Building a trusted ecosystem for sustainable food and essentials

Who We Are

Led by Suraj Bhole, an Economics postgraduate, The Smart Green combines economic efficiency, local sourcing, and modern supply-chain management to solve common problems such as inconsistent quality, price fluctuations, and unreliable delivery.

We work closely with local farmers, wholesalers, and trusted suppliers to ensure freshness, transparency, and long-term partnerships.

Our mission is to build a trusted food and essentials supply ecosystem for both businesses and households.

Our Values

Our Mission

  • Support local farmers and eco-friendly suppliers

Our Vision
